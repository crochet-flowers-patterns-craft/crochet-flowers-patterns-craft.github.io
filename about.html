Crochet Craft

A little garden, one stitch at a time

Welcome to Crochet Craft, a botanical journal for people who find a little happiness in a ball of yarn.

Our library brings together flowers, layered roses, tiny leaves, harvest motifs, and garden creatures. Each guide includes its own materials, techniques, finishing notes, and illustrations, so you can choose a project that speaks to you.

Make room for making

Choose a flower you love, read through its guide, and enjoy the process. Different yarns and tensions give each project its own character. The source and stitch terminology for each pattern are described in its article.
